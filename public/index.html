Frequently asked questions

Do I have to pay to apply?

Payment follows relevant verification and role confirmation. Keep the receipt and case reference after payment.

Who pays for my visa and travel?

Under H-2A and H-2B rules, employers generally cover visa fees and inbound travel costs, either upfront or by reimbursing you. Your written offer explains exactly how this works.

How do I know a job is real?

Every listing has an approved labor certification. You can search it yourself on the Department of Labor's public SeasonalJobs.dol.gov site.

How long does the process take?

The overall process commonly takes 2 to 3 months from job selection to travel preparation, depending on employer readiness, document accuracy, petition processing, embassy scheduling, and applicant response time..
